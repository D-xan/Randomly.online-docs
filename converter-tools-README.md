## What are the Randomly.online converters?

They are 28 unit converters covering everyday measures, the main physical quantities, and the handful of single-pair conversions people look up constantly. Each one runs in your browser, so there is no request behind the answer and no wait.

The distinguishing choice here is that the conversion factor is printed next to the result. 1 inch is 2.54 cm exactly, 1 mph is 1.609344 km/h exactly, and 1 g is 9.80665 m/s2 by definition. Where a factor is exact by definition the page says so, and where a value is a rounded convention rather than a definition it says that too. A converter that only prints a number teaches you nothing and cannot be checked.

## How precise are the results?

Every factor used is either exact by international definition or the standard published value, and the page states which, so you can check the arithmetic instead of trusting it. Rounding is applied to what is displayed, not to the factor.

Where rounding matters to the answer, you get more than one form. The cm to inches page gives the decimal and the nearest common fraction, because a workshop measurement wants sixteenths and a spreadsheet wants 3.937.
